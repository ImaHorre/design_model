# Stage 2 Physics

*Droplet bulb formation, necking, and pinch-off - surface-tension-dominated process*

## Core Physics Concept

**Process**: Droplet growth to critical radius followed by necking and snap-off
- **Driving force**: [[Pressure Variables]] - P_j (junction pressure) and [[Surface Tension]]
- **Rate-limiting**: Critical radius achievement and neck evolution
- **Time scale**: Surface tension dominated, geometry sensitive

## Key Connections

**[[Critical Radius]]**:
- Geometry-dependent snap-off condition
- Channel aspect ratio and dimension scaling
- Less sensitive to [[Nodal Network]] pressure variations

**[[Outer Phase Necking]]**:
- V3 physics correction: water viscosity dominance (not oil)
- Proper Ohnesorge number calculation
- Inertial vs viscous regime transitions

**[[Two-Stage Physics]]**:
- Distinct from [[Stage 1 Physics]] (flow-dominated)
- Initiated when meniscus reaches junction
- More uniform timing across device positions

## Research Areas

**Physics Enhancement**:
- Predictive neck instability for advanced timing control
- Multi-phase flow dynamics in junction region
- [[Contact Angle]] effects on critical radius determination

**Experimental Validation**:
- [[V5.30 Data]] Stage 2 timing uniformity observations
- Geometry scaling studies for critical radius
- Surface treatment effects on snap-off conditions
