# Stage 1 Physics

*Oil meniscus movement from reset position to junction edge - flow-driven process*

## Core Physics Concept

**Process**: Oil meniscus advancement through rung microchannel resistance
- **Driving force**: [[Pressure Variables]] - DP_rung (pressure difference across rung)
- **Rate-limiting**: Poiseuille flow through rectangular microchannel
- **Time scale**: Flow-dominated, viscosity and pressure sensitive

## Key Connections

**[[Nodal Network]]**:
- DP_rung derived from device-scale pressure distribution
- Network position effects on local driving pressure
- Distance from inlets correlates with Stage 1 timing

**[[Viscosity Correction]]**:
- Experimental factor ~3-5× slower than simple Poiseuille prediction
- Potential mechanisms: [[Contact Angle]] dynamics, [[Surface Effects]]
- Dynamic wetting and fresh interface formation

**[[Two-Stage Physics]]**:
- Distinct from [[Stage 2 Physics]] (surface-tension-dominated)
- Completes when meniscus reaches junction edge
- Feeds into Stage 2 initial conditions

## Research Areas

**Physics Enhancement**:
- Surface activation mechanisms and dynamic wetting
- Marangoni stress effects and [[Surfactant Effects]]
- Non-ideal flow behavior in microchannels

**Experimental Validation**:
- [[V5.30 Data]] correlations with oil inlet pressure
- Position-dependent timing variations
- Viscosity and surface treatment sensitivity studies
