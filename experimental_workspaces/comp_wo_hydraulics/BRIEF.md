## Purpose

Model-only exploration of hydraulic pressure profiles and rung flow distributions across device
geometries and operating conditions. No experimental data comparison.

Covers:
- W/O pressure and flow profile sweeps on W11 device (varying Pd and Qc)
- O/W W11 layout and pressure check
- V5-30 geometry pressure sweep
- 30µm test geometry rung-length sweeps

## Files

- `figures/` — W/O sweep overview and pressure profile plots across all Pd/Qc conditions
- `wo_w11/` — W/O W11 device figures (8 canonical + CLI pressure_sweep variant)
- `ow_w11/` — O/W W11 device layout and pressure sweep
- `v5_30_report/` — V5-30 model pressure sweep
- `wo_test30um/` — 30µm geometry rung-length sweeps
